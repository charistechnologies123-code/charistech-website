About Charis Technologies

Building a technologically empowered nation by nurturing tech prodigies and equipping every citizen to harness the power of innovation.

Who We Are

Charis Technologies is a purpose-driven Nigerian technology brand committed to raising the next generation of tech prodigies and empowering everyday people to thrive in a digital world. We believe technology is not only the future, it's a transformative tool for national development across every sector: from information and communication to energy, agriculture, education, and beyond.

Rooted in innovation, inclusivity, and impact, Charis Technologies offers hands-on tech training, solutions, and services designed to equip individuals and businesses to compete, create, and contribute meaningfully in today's fast-paced, tech-driven society.

Our Vision

To build a technologically empowered nation by nurturing tech prodigies and equipping every citizen to harness the power of innovation across all fields of technology, thereby driving national transformation and global relevance.

Our Mission

  • Identify, mentor, and develop young talents into world-class tech innovators and leaders.
  • Bridge the digital divide by making technology accessible and understandable to everyday Nigerians, regardless of background or education level.
  • Facilitate the adoption and integration of cutting-edge technologies, from information and communication tech to engineering, energy, and agricultural technologies, to solve real-world problems.
  • Collaborate with communities, schools, and organizations to foster a culture of innovation, inclusivity, and sustainable development through technology.
  • Propel Nigeria’s advancement by creating a generation that doesn’t just use technology but pioneers it.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Faith

Belief and total reliance in Almighty God, dependence on His word and prayers as the foundation of our work.

Innovation

We embrace creativity and forward-thinking to develop groundbreaking solutions that meet the evolving needs of our nation.

Empowerment

We believe in unlocking the potential of every individual by providing the tools, knowledge, and support to thrive in a tech-driven world.

Excellence

We are committed to delivering high standards in everything we do — from mentorship to technological solutions.

Integrity

We operate with honesty, transparency, and ethical practices in all our engagements and decisions.

Inclusivity

We strive to ensure that everyone, regardless of background or status, has equal access to opportunities in technology.

Impact

We are focused on creating real, measurable change in lives, communities, and the nation through technology.

Collaboration

We value partnerships and teamwork — across sectors, disciplines, and generations — to achieve shared goals.

Lifelong Learning

We foster a culture of continuous improvement and learning, recognizing that growth in technology requires adaptability and curiosity.

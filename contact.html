Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enroll in a program?

You can enroll by using the register button the programs page, calling us, or sending a WhatsApp message. We'll guide you through the registration process and provide all necessary information.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes! We understand that affordability is important. We offer flexible payment plans for most of our programs. Contact us to discuss options that work for you.

Can I visit your facility before enrolling?

Absolutely! We encourage prospective students and parents to visit our tech hub. Please schedule an appointment by contacting us in advance.

Do you provide certificates?

Yes, we provide industry-relevant certificates upon successful completion of all our training programs. These certificates validate your skills and knowledge.

What are your class sizes?

We maintain small class sizes to ensure personalized attention and effective learning. Most classes have 15-20 students maximum, allowing instructors to give individual support.
